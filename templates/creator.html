What I believe about learning to code

Typing beats reading

Understanding a concept and being able to produce it are different skills. Only one of them gets you a working program, and only practice builds it.

Feedback has a shelf life

Every lesson ends with code you run and output that is checked immediately, while the idea is still in your head and a correction still lands.

Forgetting is the real problem

Most courses are built to be finished, not remembered. Smart Review brings concepts back on a schedule so what you learn survives the week.
